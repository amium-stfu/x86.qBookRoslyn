## Evidence and reproduction

`DialogWithParameters.Show()` calls `UpdateLayout()`, which calls `CreateContents()` on first use. `CreateContents()` adds parameter boxes and two buttons through `DialogBox.Add(UIBox, ...)`. That method creates the UIBox and calls `frame.Add(box.Frame)`. Both frames are `Box`, so the invoked overload is `Box.Add(Box)` in `qbookCsScript/Controls/Box.cs`.

Before attachment, that overload snapshots `Root.ControlDict`, adds Control targets from `ItemDestroyHandler.Destroyers`, and traverses their ownership trees. The local `List<Control>` is searched with `visited.Any(c => ReferenceEquals(c, container))` on each dequeue. Repeated lookups can produce quadratic work. `Box.OwnedControls()` includes both `Children` and `Boxes`, allowing duplicate queue entries. After the scan, attachment updates `Boxes`, sets `Parent` (which registers `Children`), assigns the directory throughout the child tree, and invalidates bounds. These latter operations must be preserved.
